## Market research → design hypotheses (not validated by merge)

- **Positioning.** The market already offers model lists and hardware-fit tools (MR-01/02/03). The research synthesis ([#47](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/issues/47)) recommends “a bounded decision workflow with evidence, uncertainty and a shareable configuration record”. The site therefore leads with **traceable compatibility, explicit unknowns and a shareable record**, not catalogue size. In line with [#57](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/issues/57), we do not market completeness we have not measured.
- **Personas** ([#52](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/issues/52), desk hypotheses only). The UI serves AI application developers and infrastructure engineers first: dense tables, settings, rule ids. Beginners get need badges, a next-action hint and starter stacks.
- **Terminology.** We use “stack”/“build”, “evidence”, “provenance” and “Unknown”, never “compatible” for missing data. “Hard incompatibility” (Fail) is kept separate from “missing evidence” (Unknown), as [#51](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/issues/51) hypothesis 3 suggests.
- **Trust.** Every synthetic item and price is labelled. Totals show “≥ $X + unpriced” when coverage is incomplete. Sources and freshness appear on detail pages. There are no affiliate links, ratings or vote counts.
- **Pivot watch.** If MR validation ([#63](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/issues/63), [#66](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/issues/66)) narrows the product to a local-versus-API decision tool, the same contract, data layer and builder can host a reduced T0–T2 flow. Nothing here assumes the full T0–T6 surface ships at once.

## Scoped freshness and source-use acceptance

[PR #127](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/pull/127) merged on 2026-10-18 at 00:19:21 UTC as `92111483b8d52b1b05152391b2d73e9f59ca2fba`. Its [catalog source record](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/blob/92111483b8d52b1b05152391b2d73e9f59ca2fba/research/T0-configuration/catalog-data-sources.md) and the [#41 receiving receipt for T4](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/issues/41#issuecomment-6242626857) establish the following scoped intake policy:

- Source-checked claims without independent support use v1 `unverified`. New `verified` entries require linked independent corroboration or reproduction. Historical `verified` values are not proof of independent support; inspect the claim evidence rather than mass-relabeling records.
- Version-pinned facts do not expire simply with age. Unversioned hosted services and remote MCP servers are current-state facts: use the capture date as `version`, retain the selected snapshot, and show the evidence date. No TTL is adopted. A newer snapshot can trigger re-checking the selected tuple but does not by itself invalidate a build.
- Source-checked means the cited text was actually read. Unrendered cells remain unknown; record the retrieval method when using a text extractor.
- Apply the [#60 per-source rights table](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/blob/92111483b8d52b1b05152391b2d73e9f59ca2fba/research/market/rights-validation.md). A source without a row permits research-note paraphrase with a link only; automated retrieval, display and redistribution remain `no` until recorded terms permit them. MCP registry metadata reuse is scoped to its row; a listing is not a security certification.

The [#60 model-metadata receipt](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/issues/60#issuecomment-6242698806) accepts that restrictive intake handoff with attribution, license and source/use constraints. It explicitly grants neither collection execution nor broad rights to weights, cards, measurements or derivative content. Other consumers still require their own dispositions.

This is not global research acceptance or website production integration. Price-specific freshness/invalidation, region/currency and price conflicts remain open with [#39](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/issues/39)/[#41](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/issues/41); versioned policy encoding remains under [#40](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/issues/40). The website at `dc66a9e` used a provisional 30-day preview warning. The website now displays capture dates without age-based freshness claims, and a newer snapshot is informational. Item revision mismatches, missing items, build schema mismatches and explicit price validity remain separate checks. Real-data adoption still requires retained-snapshot, correction and source-use acceptance without editing frozen v1.
